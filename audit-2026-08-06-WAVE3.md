## 1. CRITICAL

### F1 — `fp32_escalate` is unconditional fp32: the escalation trigger cannot fire. **OPEN**

`vfe3/families/gaussian.py:711` gates escalation on `bool((~ok).any())`, where `ok` comes from
`safe_cholesky(..., rounds=5)`. That ladder adds an **absolute** ridge `eps*10^t` (1e-6…1e-2,
`vfe3/numerics.py:230`) to every failed element, which repairs any fp32 rounding-induced loss of
positive-definiteness. Verified independently: across `lam_max ∈ {1,4,100}` and `cond ∈ {1e4…1e10}`,
**every** fp32 PD loss was repaired at `t=0`; 75/75 at cond 1e9, 141/141 at 1e10; **zero** PD losses
at any scale below cond 1e8. So `ok` is all-True and the branch is dead code in practice.

Cholesky failure is not a proxy for accuracy — the two are decoupled by ~6 orders of conditioning.
Measured fp32-vs-fp64 KL error at K=20: `cond=1e6` → median 3.4e-2 / max 3.7e-1; `cond=1e7` →
median 3.4e-1 / p95 1.97 / max 8.24. Through `beta = softmax(-E/tau)` with `tau=sqrt(10)`, a 0.75
error is a **1.27x** attention-weight ratio (the as-filed "2.7x" was arithmetically wrong and is
corrected here).

**Severity qualifier the challenge established:** at the *trained* operating point the repo's own
recorded conditioning is `cond(Omega Sigma Omega^T)` **median 2.7e3 / p95 2.1e4**
(`vfe3/config.py:854-856`), where I measure ΔKL median 9.1e-5 / p95 6.4e-4 → beta ratio 1.0000 /
1.0002. **The documented 4e-6 bound is correct at the operating point.** The exposure is that the
policy has no guard if conditioning ever leaves that band — and `e_sigma_q_trust=10` permits a
one-step excursion of 20 nats (F7), so it can.

*Fix direction:* key escalation on an accuracy proxy computable in the working dtype — e.g.
`diag(L).min()^2 / diag(L).max()^2` as a free Cholesky-based conditioning estimate — rather than on
`ok`. Numerics-changing; gate it.

### F2 — The fp64 congruence is built and immediately thrown away. **OPEN** *(adjudicated)*

Two agents reached opposite conclusions here; the adjudication resolved it.

**The fact both agreed on:** `vfe3/geometry/transport.py:2377/2387/2405` promotes the transported
covariance to float64 (`retain_full_precision=True` → `sigma.double()`), and
`vfe3/families/gaussian.py:686-690` then computes `compute_dtype` from `_public_dtype` (fp32 under
`fp32_escalate`) and does `sigma_t = other.sigma.to(compute_dtype)`. A 629 MB fp64 tensor is
produced and rounded straight back to fp32. The escalation at `:713` compounds it by calling
`.double()` on the *already-rounded* fp32 values, recovering nothing.

**Adjudication.** Agent NUM proposed keying `compute_dtype` on `other.sigma.dtype` to keep fp64 —
**rejected**, that silently disables the `full_cov_kl_precision` knob entirely. Agent PERF proposed
dropping the fp64 production as bit-identical — **the conclusion is right but the stated reason is
wrong**: producing `Omega Sigma Omega^T` in fp32 *arithmetic* is not the same as producing it in
fp64 and rounding (0/60 bitwise identical, median relative difference ~7.4e-8). It happens not to
matter, because every full-covariance congruence in `transport.py` hard-codes fp64 *arithmetic*
independently of `retain_full_precision` (`:2736`, `:2744`, `:2751`, `:2884-2886`,
`:2600-2621`) — the flag changes only the output dtype, and fp32→fp64 promotion is exact. Simulating
the actual two branches (round early vs round late): **0/300 mismatches, bit-identical.**

*Correct fix:* thread the active `full_cov_kl_precision()` into `FullGaussian.transport_dispersion`
so `retain_full_precision = (the KL will actually compute in fp64)` — False under `fp32_escalate`,
True under `"fp64"`. **BIT-IDENTICAL under both policies.** Saves ~1 GB of traffic and 315 MB of peak
per traversal.

NUM's 195/200-indefiniteness scenario reproduces (193/200) but at effective `cond ≈ 1e8` with
`||phi||_F=6` — four to five decades off the live path, where init is isotropic `4I` (cond = 1).

### F3 — The full-covariance congruence is an unconditional fp64 island with no toggle. **OPEN**

`vfe3/geometry/transport.py:2736,2744,2751` hard-code `.double()` on `omega_blocks` and `sigma_diag`
in `_compact_factored_full_covariance`; same pattern at `:2884-2886` and `:2600-2621`. There is no
dtype parameter, no module global and no autocast guard reaching any of them — verified by reading
the signatures and grepping for a toggle. Unlike the KL (`full_cov_kl_precision`) and unlike the
diagonal sibling's `_escalate_if_negative`, this stage has no policy at all.

Cost: the `blocks_only` einsum is `2·2·B·N²·H·d³` = **1.573 GFLOP in fp64** over ~1.57 GB of traffic,
arithmetic intensity 0.83 FLOP/byte — on an RTX 5090 whose fp64 rate is ~1/64 of fp32. This is now
the single largest stage in the step. In fp32 the same work is 0.79 GB and 0.15 ms of FLOPs.

*Fix direction:* add `full_cov_congruence_precision = "fp64" | "fp32_escalate"` mirroring the KL
knob; the escalation predicate already exists next door at `transport.py:1412`
(`_escalate_if_negative`) and needs an SPD rather than a sign trigger. Numerics-changing; gate it,
default `"fp64"` so nothing on disk changes until the user opts in. Expected 2–8x on this stage.

## 2. HIGH

### F5 — The `skip_step` guard has no cumulative counter, and skip rate is arm-dependent. **OPEN**
*(re-scoped by the challenge — the as-filed version overstated this)*

`vfe3/train.py:725` sets `skip_step` on a non-finite gradient; `:743-750` zeroes grads; `:806-809`
gates `successful_updates += 1; scheduler.step()` on `did_step`. Because the LR scheduler clocks on
accepted updates, the LR trajectory inverts to a skip count exactly.

**Confirmed via two independent channels.** `pickletools` on the terminal checkpoint gives
`successful_updates: 9554` at `step: 10000` for the `e_q_mu_lr=0.05` arm; back-solving the LambdaLR
clock from that arm's own `lr_mu` column gives the same 446. `successful_updates` is genuinely an
accepted-step count — single write site, seeded to 0 on a fresh run, `run_artifacts.py:1006`
enforces `<= step`, `grad_accum_steps=1`, `resume_from=None`.

**Two corrections the challenge forced, both material:**
1. **It is not silent.** `train.py:760` writes `metrics_out["step_skipped"]` and it is persisted
   (`:1893`); `grad_norm*` go blank on skipped rows. The historical `832.33` run had
   `step_skipped == 1.0` on **597 of 600** rows. What is missing is a *cumulative* field in
   `summary.json` and any sampling between log intervals — `summary.json` reports
   `n_steps: cfg.max_steps` (`run_artifacts.py:3037`), the configured value, never the realized one.
2. **The live rate is 0.2%, not 4.5%.** The completed `e_q_mu_lr=0.1` arm back-solves to **19 skips
   of 10000**. The 4.46% belongs to the `0.05` arm.

**The real research risk** is therefore not silence but **arm-dependent budget loss**: 9554 vs ~9981
accepted updates across arms of the same sweep means arms are compared at different effective
training budgets and different points on the LR schedule.

Historical worst case, for calibration: `vfe3_runs/832.33_wikitext-103_K30_block_glk_s6` completed
**453 accepted updates of 60000 (99.245% skipped)** over 11.4 hours and published
`summary.json` with `n_steps: 60000, test_ppl: 832.33, best_step: 15000`. `loss_finite` was 1.0 on
all 600 rows and `nonfinite_frac` 0.0 throughout — the forward was finite, the backward blew up. That
config had `e_q_mu_lr=0.9` with `e_mu_q_trust=None` (no mean trust region at all); the live
`0.5`/`ball` is the mitigation and is only partial.

**PR #219 does not fix this.** Its `train.py` hunk only drops `snapshot=` from `_val_diagnostics`.
It adds `retraction.py:566 nonfinite_tangent_elements()` — which has **zero call sites in `vfe3/`**,
i.e. a dead counter. Wiring it is part of the fix.

*Fix direction:* cumulative skip counter in the `train()` loop (free — `status_out["did_step"]` is
already returned every step), surfaced in `summary.json` as accepted-update count and fraction; warn
on the first skip and on runs of >10 consecutive; wire `nonfinite_tangent_elements()`; and record
per-arm `successful_updates` in `sweep_results.csv` so arms are comparable.

### F6 — Every `val_*` diagnostic is one fixed 64-token sequence, and `estep_f_nondecreasing_frac` is a single Bernoulli draw. **OPEN**

Separately, `train.py:1088-1089` computes `float((f[1:] > f[:-1] + 1e-9).float().mean())` and

### F7 — `e_sigma_q_trust=10.0` is wider than the entire admissible eigenvalue band. **OPEN**

`vfe3/geometry/retraction.py:674-676` bounds `||R||_F <= 10`, and `Sigma_new = S^½ exp(R) S^½`, so
one AIRM step spans eigenvalue factors `[e^-10, e^10]` = **20 nats** — against
`log(sigma_max/eps) = log(100/1e-6) = 18.42` nats for the whole admissible band. From
`sigma_init=4`, one step reaches `4e^10 = 8.8e4` (clipped to 100) or `1.8e-4`; `cond(exp R)` alone
reaches `e^20 = 4.85e8`. The `[eps, sigma_max]` projection is the only thing actually limiting it,
and that projection has hard 0/1 derivatives.

This is the mechanism that makes F1 reachable: cond ≈ 1e8 is a **one-step** excursion, and 1e8 is
where the fp32 KL error becomes material.

There is no runtime visibility: `condition_number` (`vfe3/numerics.py:289`) and its
`@register_monitor` wrapper `_mon_cond` (`:400-403`) have **zero production call sites** — `run_monitors`
(`:406`) is never called anywhere in `vfe3/`. Both are dead in production.

Sub-result, worth recording as *not* a hazard: the `exp_bounded` window `[-50, 50]`
(`retraction.py:619-620`) that Wave 2 flagged is **provably inert** under `trust=10`, since
`|lambda_i(R)| <= ||R||_F = 10 < 50`. Its zero-derivative edges are unreachable on the live path.

*Fix direction:* `e_sigma_q_trust <= 2` (≈4 nats/step) and log `condition_number(sigma_q).max()`
per step — the instrument already exists, it just has no caller.

### F8 — `alibi_slope` is a multiplier on the Press schedule, not a slope; head 1's positional prior is worth a quarter of a nat. **OPEN**

`vfe3/attention_prior.py:39-48` computes `h = arange(1, count+1); pow(2.0, -8.0*h/count)` then
returns `base * slopes`, where `base` is the config's `alibi_slope`. At `alibi_slope=1, H=2` the
**realized** per-head slopes are `2^-4 = 0.0625` and `2^-8 = 0.00390625`. Over N=64 (max distance 63)
the total positional bias spans **3.94 nats (head 0)** and **0.246 nats (head 1)**. Head 1's
`causal_alibi_noself` is numerically indistinguishable from `causal_noself`.

Two consequences. First, the prior does *not* dominate the divergence term — the concern runs the
other way, and matching head 0's row-63 prior spread needs only 3.59 nats of energy spread. Second,
**an `n_heads` sweep at fixed `alibi_slope` silently rescales the positional prior by up to 180x**:
head-0 spans are 0.246 / 3.94 / 15.75 / 28.53 / 31.50 / 44.55 nats at H = 1/2/4/7/8/16.

*Fix direction:* rename to `alibi_slope_base` and document; for any H sweep, pin the *realized*
head-0 slope rather than the base.

### F11 — `gaussian_full` at this config is provably block-diagonal, and the decode CE is exactly blind to every off-diagonal entry. **OPEN (scope/documentation, not a bug)**

Four sub-claims, all independently confirmed:
- `vfe3/model/prior_bank.py:1567` returns `torch.diag_embed(...)` at `prior_source="token"`, and
  `sigma_log_embed` is `(V, 20)` — **there are no off-diagonal parameters at all**. The packed
  lower-triangular table `s_sigma_lower_embed` exists only on the `model_channel` branch.
- `vfe3/free_energy.py:218-219` slices `q_b.block(h*d,(h+1)*d)`, so the coupling reads only the two
  head-diagonal 10x10 blocks.
- `retract_spd_full` is matmuls and spectral maps only, hence block-preserving. The sibling caveat
  that `_eigh_damped` on the degenerate `4I` spectrum returns head-mixing eigenvectors **does not
  defeat this**: because `f(lambda)` is constant across the degenerate cluster, the mixing cancels
  exactly (measured cross-head leak in `Sigma^½`: 7.3e-8 on exact `4I`, **exactly 0.0** with a 1e-6
  jitter that produces genuinely mixed eigenvectors). And there is no accumulation channel — at
  `L=1, T=1` there is one retraction per forward and `Sigma_q` is re-encoded from the `(V,K)` table
  every time.
- `prior_bank.py:1191` `per_pos = K + logdet_q` is v-independent, so it cancels algebraically exactly
  in `logsumexp_v - target_logit` (`:1241`). Perturbing `Sigma_q[0,1]` by 0.7 changes CE by 0.0;
  `Sigma_q[0,10]` by 0.7 gives 1.4e-14. `max|dCE/dSigma_diag| / max|dCE/dSigma_offdiag| ≈ 4.5e9`.

**What `gaussian_full` actually buys at this config, exactly two things:** (i) within-head 10x10
correlation, and (ii) an *untruncated* transported key covariance `Omega_h diag(sigma_j^h) Omega_h^T`
in the pair energy, instead of the diagonal path's `sum_l Omega_kl^2 s_l` truncation. Not 20x20
correlation, and nothing in the decode.

*Fix direction:* state the scope in the manuscript; if the claim is about full-rank belief
propagation, that needs `T>=2` or `L>=2`, or a token prior with a packed Cholesky.

### F12 — The self-coupling sector is identically zero at `T=1`. **OPEN (experiment design)**

`vfe3/model/model.py:1145-1146` calls `vfe_stack(beliefs, beliefs.mu, beliefs.sigma, ...)` against
the signature `vfe_stack(belief, mu_p, sigma_p, ...)` (`stack.py:31-34`) — so `p_i` is *literally the
same tensor object* as `q_i^(0)`. SymPy on the full Gaussian KL evaluated at `q = p`: value `0`,
`d/dmu` = `[0,0]`, `d/dSigma` = `[0,0,0]` — exact zeros. With `n_e_steps=1` the self term and both
its gradients vanish at the only iterate.

Consequence: `lambda_alpha=1`, `b0`, `c0` and the entire alpha-form are **inert instruments** at
`T=1`, and no prior-anchoring counterforce is exercised — which also voids the rank-collapse
discussion for this configuration. Nothing breaks the identity: `prior_handoff_rho/sigma` are dead at
`n_layers=1` (`config.py:3090-3096`), `emission_mode="off"`, `norm_type_block="none"`,
`s_e_step=False`. Second-order safety too: since `mu` and `mu_p` are the same tensor,
`autograd.grad(F, mu_q)` picks up `∂/∂q + ∂/∂p`, also identically zero along `q=p`.

*Fix direction:* no code change; run `T>=2` before reporting any alpha-sweep, and say so in the
manuscript.

### F14 — The `blocks_only` fast path allocates and scatters the full dense output. **OPEN**
