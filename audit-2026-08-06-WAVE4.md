## Headline Results

### H1. Σ is rate-limited, not blocked — and the covariance metric is measuring the embedding table

Executed on `best_model.pt`, real test tokens (B=8, N=64):

```
ONE E-STEP relative displacement
   mu    : ||dmu||/||mu||   = 1.32342
   Sigma : ||dS||_F/||S||_F = 0.11405        ratio 11.6  ~=  e_q_mu_lr/e_q_sigma_lr = 12
tr(Sigma) ENTRY (per-token table lookup): mean 52.67347  CV 0.075728
tr(Sigma) EXIT  (post single E-step)     : mean 50.28004  CV 0.067031
corr(entry, exit) = 0.961307   R^2 = 0.924111
```

The Σ retraction is `R_i = -e_q_sigma_lr · (Σ_i^{1/2} P̄_i Σ_i^{1/2} − I)`, so `e_q_sigma_lr` is
literally *the fraction of the log-gap to the Σ fixed point closed per iteration* — dimensionless,
directly comparable to `e_q_mu_lr`. The 12× config ratio is a real inference-rate asymmetry.

Mechanisms **ruled out** by execution:
- *Gradient blocked* — Σ receives **8.5× more** gradient than μ (`sigma_log_embed.grad` norm 1.287e+01
  vs `mu_embed.grad` 1.514e+00).
- *Trust region binding* — `||R||_F` mean 6.15e-02, p95 8.63e-02, max 9.42e-02 against
  `e_sigma_q_trust=1`: binds on **0.00%**, 10× headroom.
- *Decode ignores Σ* — Σ→batch-mean costs **+2.799 nats**, so Σ is worth 2.8 nats. But
  Σ_exit→Σ_entry costs only **+0.0405 nats**: 98.6% of Σ's value is the embedding table, 1.4% is
  inference.

**More E-steps cannot fix it.** The Σ fixed point is precision fusion,
`Σ_i^{-1} = Σ_j β_ij (Ω_ij Σ_j Ω_ij^T)^{-1}` — a β-weighted harmonic mean of the *context's*
covariances, hence a contraction on the across-token covariance field. Closed form verified against
autograd to `2.776e-16`. Depth sweep 0→8 on the trained checkpoint:

```
CV(tr Sigma): 0.07573 0.06703 0.06538 0.06605 0.06744 0.06916 0.07104 0.07301 0.07503
```

Never leaves a ±10% band around the table's own 0.0757.

**The gate is invalid.** `run_artifacts.py:2716-2718` gates on `sigma_trace_cv > 0.10` computed from
post-forward `out.sigma`. The one run on disk that PASSES — `vfe3_runs/138.35_fisher`,
`sigma_trace_cv=0.21570` — sets **`skip_belief_sigma_update: True`** in its own config: it passed the
covariance-liveness gate by deleting the covariance inference. Its spread came from
`m_p_sigma_lr=0.01` (10× the reference) via the model channel.

**The real bottleneck is the M-step table.** After 60000 steps `mean tr Σ` moved **+1.42%** from its
`sigma_init=2.5` initialization (50.000 → 50.711), at `m_p_sigma_lr=0.001` vs `m_p_mu_lr=0.015`.

### H6. `laplace_full` is not well-posed as a closed-form divergence family

Four independent routes, each closed by derivation:

1. **Bessel-K symmetric multivariate Laplace** (Kotz–Kozubowski–Podgórski 2001, the
   Exp-mixture-of-Gaussians form): the density carries `K_ν(√(2Q))`, so even the differential entropy
   needs `E[log K_ν]`. The most recent comprehensive catalogue of exact multivariate KL expressions —
   Nawa & Nadarajah, *Entropy* **26**(8) (2024) 663, covering 20+ multivariate and matrix-variate
   families — does not include it.
2. **Elliptical `exp(−√Q)`** (multivariate generalized Gaussian at β=½): normalizer and entropy *are*
   closed form, but `KL(q‖p) = −H(q) + log Z_p + E_q[√((x−μ_p)ᵀΣ_p⁻¹(x−μ_p))]` and the cross term has
   no closed form for `Σ_p ≠ cΣ_q`. The best published exact expression (ibid. §2.2) is a nested
   **infinite triple sum** and assumes a **common location** — precisely the case the pair energy
   never sees, since `Ω_ij # q_j` carries a transported mean.
3. **The Gaussian-scale-mixture upper bound is vacuous.** With a shared `W ~ Exp(1)`, the chain rule
   gives `KL ≤ ½[tr(Σ_p⁻¹Σ_q) − K + log(|Σ_p|/|Σ_q|)] + ½·E[1/W]·ΔᵀΣ_p⁻¹Δ`, and
   `E[1/W] = ∫₀^∞ w⁻¹e^{−w}dw = +∞` (verified numerically: the truncated integral over `[1e-8,1]` is
   already 17.62 and grows as `−log ε`). Finite only at equal locations.
4. **The linear-image family** `X = μ + A Z`, `Z ~ ∏Laplace(0,b)` *is* closed under GL(K) congruence
   (`A → ΩA`), unlike the fixed-coordinate product Laplace. But with `A_q ≠ Ω_ij A_j` the integrand is
   piecewise log-linear on an arrangement of `2d` hyperplanes, so the divergence is a sum over
   `Σ_{i≤d} C(2d,i)` polyhedral cones: **616,666 at d_head = 10** and **6.19e11 at K = 20**. Formally
   closed form, computationally dead.

The two frames coincide in exactly one case — the Regime-I coboundary `Ω_ij = U_i U_j⁻¹` with
`A_i = U_i A` — at which point the divergence collapses to the exact diagonal formula at diagonal
cost. That is the `gaussian_frame_diagonal` construction, and per `ablation.py:262-267`'s own note
the gauge then **cancels out of the pair energy**.

> **Recommendation:** build `laplace_frame_diagonal` (exact, cheap, GL(K)-closed) if a heavy-tailed
> family is wanted, accepting that under a flat cocycle the gauge leaves the coupling — a different
> model, not a refinement. Otherwise keep the Gaussian for the full-covariance sector. An honest
> `laplace_full` would need a stochastic or series estimator, and a stochastic pair energy breaks the
> deterministic softmax stationarity that `free_energy.py:378-385` relies on.

### H7. The natural-gradient concern from 2026-08-05 is retracted

Re-derived from scratch and verified numerically (`retraction.py:880-921`):

- `nat_mu = Σ ∇_μ` vs `Σ@G`: relative **4.4e-17**. Against a finite-difference Fisher built from the
  exact Gaussian KL (K=6, h=1e-4): `‖F⁻¹G − nat_mu‖/‖nat_mu‖ = 5.6e-7`, at the `O(h)` truncation
  floor; `F` itself matches `Σ⁻¹` to 5.2e-7.
- `nat_Σ = 2 Σ ∇_Σ Σ` symmetrized vs `2SGS`: relative **8.6e-17**. Metric identity
  `½tr(S⁻¹ nat_Σ S⁻¹ V) − ⟨G_Σ,V⟩ = −1.78e-15` against `|rhs| = 4.666`, so the Σ block uses the
  Gaussian Fisher `g(dS₁,dS₂) = ½tr(S⁻¹dS₁S⁻¹dS₂)` — **exactly one half AIRM** (ratio computed
  0.500000), the established project result.
- **The Σ step is not Euclidean-then-project.** `spd_affine → retract_spd_full` computes
  `Σ_new = S^½ exp(S^{-½}(−lr·nat_Σ)S^{-½}) S^½`. A constant metric rescale leaves the Levi-Civita
  connection unchanged, so `exp^{Fisher} = exp^{AIRM}` for the same tangent — this is exactly the
  Fisher exponential map applied to the Fisher gradient.
- The μ step is additive, which *is* the Fisher geodesic on the μ-slice (the metric `Σ⁻¹` is constant
  in μ), and the ball-mode trust region bounds `‖L_q⁻¹δμ‖₂`, the Fisher norm of the step.

**Metric used == metric claimed on both arms. No mismatch exists.**

## Recommended Config Changes

Ranked by expected value per unit of risk. Each is tied to the measurement that motivates it.

1. **`max_steps: 60000 → 120000`** (retune cosine over the new horizon). Motivated by H5. Expected
   `val_ppl` 123–132 vs 146.03, for ~2.6 GPU-hours total. Highest-confidence win available.
2. **`m_p_sigma_lr: 0.001 → 0.005–0.008` and `e_q_sigma_lr: 0.025 → 0.08–0.10` together.** Motivated

## Deferred / Not Done

- **A well-posed extension the code does not offer: per-BLOCK alpha.** Under `gauge_group="block_glk"`
  + `prior_source="token"` the belief covariance is provably block-diagonal (WAVE3 F11:
  `prior_bank.py:1567` `diag_embed`s a `(V,20)` table; `retract_spd_full` is matmul + spectral map,
  hence block-preserving), so `KL = Σ_h KL_h` **exactly** over the two 10-dim head blocks. Registering
  `state_dependent_per_block` with `per_block=True` would give a mathematically sound per-component
  alpha where `per_coord` cannot exist. It must be guarded on an *executed* block-diagonality
  certificate, not on `gauge_group` alone — a `model_channel` prior with a packed Cholesky
  (`s_sigma_lower_embed`) breaks the decomposition. **Not implemented; proposed.**
- **Backward-pass precision.** Every accuracy number in H4 is forward-only. The M-step gradient flows
  back through the same `solve_triangular` chain at `κ(ΩΣΩᵀ)` up to 3e5, and the backward of a
  triangular solve can be less accurate than its forward. Settling this needs an fp32-vs-fp64 adjoint
  comparison on the pair energy w.r.t. `phi`. **Open.**
- **Deserialized-config and post-construction-`setattr` paths** were out of scope for the
  reachability sweep.
- The formal `verification` ledger integration was not run; this wave substituted **executed repros
  by the investigators themselves** (checkpoint-level measurements, CUDA-event benchmarks, fp64
  reference recomputation), which is a stronger evidence standard than a source-reading verifier for
  the findings it covers. Findings marked "read" rather than "measured" in the punch list have NOT
  received that treatment and should be treated as unverified.
