## Batch 3 -- the remaining confirmed-high findings (C-04, C-05, D-02, E-01)

`transport.py`, `kernels.py`, `config.py`, `tests/test_audit_fixes_2026_07_26.py` (+18 tests, 13 of
which fail without these changes).

**C-04.** `_direct_link_diagonal_covariance` was the one diagonal-congruence route without the
autocast island, float64 escalation and `clamp(min=0.0)` its compact and factored siblings carry. Its
`r^T C_j r` regrouping is PSD in exact arithmetic and reachable below zero by cancellation; measured
at N=3, K=4 over 300 draws with `||phi||_F = 8` and an anisotropic sigma, 29/300 draws returned a
negative transported variance under bf16 autocast (worst `-2.85e+09`, relative error `4.64e+02`
against the fp64 dense reference). A negative reaches the divergence kernel's `clamp(min=eps)` and
inverts that key's precision weight by six orders. After: 0/300 negative, relative error `3.94e-03`.
The finding also reported the missing `_restore_certified_self_links_` call; that half is a no-op --
`DirectLinkTransport` carries no cocycle certification, so the helper returns early by construction.

**C-05.** The dense pairwise `Omega` einsum and the dense diagonal congruence were autocast-eligible,
so under AMP they ran in bf16 on top of vertex factors already pinned to fp32. `Omega_ii = I` is what
makes the structural self energy `E_ii = 0` and keeps the self pair out of `pair_mask`; measured
`||Omega_ii - I||_inf = 4.76e-03` under bf16 against `1.19e-07` at fp32, and `4.63e-03` relative
error on the congruence. Both now evaluate inside the same `autocast(enabled=False)` island the
factored routes use, which makes the AMP result byte-identical to fp32 (`1.19e-07`, `8.26e-08`). The
dense form is the manifestly nonnegative `sum_l Omega_kl^2 sigma_l`, so it needs no escalation or
floor -- only the working precision was missing.

**D-02.** `mm_exact_update` took `family` and `divergence_family` but never called
`uses_kernel_route`, and built its grid from `pairwise_energy` rather than `fam.coupling_energy`. So
`gaussian_diagonal_exact` returned a `mu*` bit-identical to `gaussian_diagonal` (the family seam was
bypassed entirely) and `jeffreys`/`bhattacharyya`/`squared_hellinger` drove beta and `pair_mask`
before being fused with hardcoded diagonal-Gaussian-KL precision expressions. There is no oracle for
a closed-form minimizer, so unlike the sibling `belief_gradients` this seam now fails closed. Both
live callers already gate, so no shipped config changes.

**E-01.** `_fold_gamma_prior` normalizes gamma over its own key row and only then masks the mixture to
beta's support. A convex mixture is not proportional to a single exponential, so gamma's row
normalizer does not cancel and a future key inside it changes the belief log-prior at strictly past
entries. Measured on `log_prior[i<4, j<=i]` for two sequences differing only in the last token, at
`beta='causal'`: `causal` and `causal_alibi_noself` gammas give exactly 0, against `4.8e-07`
(`uniform`, `alibi`) and `5.9e-05` (`windowed`). Under `s_e_step` a non-causal gamma also refines
`s_i` itself against future tokens, which no masking at the fold site can undo, so the combination
fails closed in `__post_init__` instead. The gate reads both supports from the registry builders at
`max_seq_len` with the same kwargs `model._attention_log_prior` passes, so a newly registered prior is
covered without editing `config.py`. Only future keys count: a gamma allowing a past key beta forbids
(the self key, where `causal_alibi_noself` and `causal` differ) shifts the mixture weight but carries
no future information, and the shipped configs pair exactly there -- they are unaffected.
