## Batch 3 -- the remaining confirmed-high findings (C-04, C-05, D-02, E-01)

`transport.py`, `kernels.py`, `config.py`, `tests/test_audit_fixes_2026_07_26.py` (+18 tests, 13 of
which fail without these changes).

**C-04.** `_direct_link_diagonal_covariance` was the one diagonal-congruence route without the
autocast island, float64 escalation and `clamp(min=0.0)` its compact and factored siblings carry. Its
`r^T C_j r` regrouping is PSD in exact arithmetic and reachable below zero by cancellation; measured
at N=3, K=4 over 300 draws with `||phi||_F = 8` and an anisotropic sigma, 29/300 draws returned a
negative transported variance under bf16 autocast (worst `-2.85e+09`, relative error `4.64e+02`
against the fp64 dense reference). A negative reaches the divergence kernel's `clamp(min=eps)` and
inverts that key's precision weight by six orders. After: 0/300 negative, relative error `3.94e-03`.
The finding also reported the missing `_restore_certified_self_links_` call; that half is a no-op --
`DirectLinkTransport` carries no cocycle certification, so the helper returns early by construction.

**C-05.** The dense pairwise `Omega` einsum and the dense diagonal congruence were autocast-eligible,
so under AMP they ran in bf16 on top of vertex factors already pinned to fp32. `Omega_ii = I` is what
makes the structural self energy `E_ii = 0` and keeps the self pair out of `pair_mask`; measured
`||Omega_ii - I||_inf = 4.76e-03` under bf16 against `1.19e-07` at fp32, and `4.63e-03` relative
error on the congruence. Both now evaluate inside the same `autocast(enabled=False)` island the
factored routes use, which makes the AMP result byte-identical to fp32 (`1.19e-07`, `8.26e-08`). The
dense form is the manifestly nonnegative `sum_l Omega_kl^2 sigma_l`, so it needs no escalation or
floor -- only the working precision was missing.

**D-02.** `mm_exact_update` took `family` and `divergence_family` but never called
`uses_kernel_route`, and built its grid from `pairwise_energy` rather than `fam.coupling_energy`. So
`gaussian_diagonal_exact` returned a `mu*` bit-identical to `gaussian_diagonal` (the family seam was
bypassed entirely) and `jeffreys`/`bhattacharyya`/`squared_hellinger` drove beta and `pair_mask`
before being fused with hardcoded diagonal-Gaussian-KL precision expressions. There is no oracle for
a closed-form minimizer, so unlike the sibling `belief_gradients` this seam now fails closed. Both
live callers already gate, so no shipped config changes.

**E-01.** `_fold_gamma_prior` normalizes gamma over its own key row and only then masks the mixture to
beta's support. A convex mixture is not proportional to a single exponential, so gamma's row
normalizer does not cancel and a future key inside it changes the belief log-prior at strictly past
entries. Measured on `log_prior[i<4, j<=i]` for two sequences differing only in the last token, at
`beta='causal'`: `causal` and `causal_alibi_noself` gammas give exactly 0, against `4.8e-07`
(`uniform`, `alibi`) and `5.9e-05` (`windowed`). Under `s_e_step` a non-causal gamma also refines
`s_i` itself against future tokens, which no masking at the fold site can undo, so the combination
fails closed in `__post_init__` instead. The gate reads both supports from the registry builders at
`max_seq_len` with the same kwargs `model._attention_log_prior` passes, so a newly registered prior is
covered without editing `config.py`. Only future keys count: a gamma allowing a past key beta forbids
(the self key, where `causal_alibi_noself` and `causal` differ) shifts the mixture weight but carries
no future information, and the shipped configs pair exactly there -- they are unaffected.

## Batch 4 -- registry capabilities (E-03, E-04, E-05)

`families/base.py`, `families/gaussian.py`, `families/exact_congruence.py`,
`families/frame_gaussian.py`, `geometry/transport.py`, `geometry/groups.py`, `config.py`,
`run_artifacts.py`, plus five test files. All three findings share one root: the family registry
carried no capability metadata, so every consumer that needed to know what a family supports either
hardcoded its name or found out at the first forward.

**E-03.** `gaussian_diagonal_exact` and `gaussian_frame_diagonal` raised mid-forward on config
pairings nothing rejected: `exact + renyi 0.5`, `exact + squared_hellinger`, `exact +
regime_ii_link`, and (from A-01, batch 2) `frame + regime_ii`. In `ablation.py` a mid-forward raise
falls past the `VFE3Config(**cfg_dict)` handler to the outer `except Exception`, so the cell is
misfiled as `error_kind="train"`. Families now declare their scope on the class --
`requires_kl_divergence` and `transport_requirement` -- and `__post_init__` enforces it. The
transport side is a three-level capability ladder declared at registration,
`PAIR_TRANSPORT_KINDS = ("opaque", "pair_inverse", "coboundary")`: `flat` builds
`Omega_ij = U_i U_j^{-1}` and declares `coboundary`; `regime_ii`/`regime_ii_covariant` return a dense
invertible operator and declare `pair_inverse`; the two link modes return a `DirectLinkTransport`
that exposes no pairwise operator at all and declare `opaque`. The level defaults to the weakest, so
a new registration must claim more deliberately. Both checks query the registries, so a newly
registered family or transport is covered without editing `config.py`.

**E-04.** Two hardcoded tuples `("gaussian_diagonal", "gaussian_full")` -- the barycenter r-update
gate and the decode-kernel gate -- rejected both 2026-07 families on the merits of neither.
`barycenter_r_` branches only on `cov_kind` and implements the Gaussian moment match; the fast decode
kernels assume the Gaussian pointwise readout. Both families override only their transport/coupling
seams and inherit `DiagonalGaussian`'s moments, self-divergence and decode verbatim, so both call
sites were already correct for them. Replaced with a declared `gaussian_pointwise_algebra`
capability, inherited by construction and False only for `laplace_diagonal`.

**E-05.** `check_admissible` -- the only executable invariance certificate in the project -- branched
on three hardcoded family names and raised `NotImplementedError` otherwise, so neither family
registered in 2026-07 could be exercised by any invariance test. That is how `gaussian_frame_diagonal`
shipped silently discarding its transport. The congruence itself is family-independent; only the
readout of the pushed covariance varies, so that is now a family hook, `from_covariance`, and the
verifier dispatches through the registry. One of the three hardcoded names, `"gaussian"`, was not a
registered family at all: it was dead data in `invariant_families`, unable to match `cfg.family` at
its one production reader, and it is dropped. A new test pins the declaration against the verifier for
every registered (group, family) pair, so the two cannot drift. Existing tests asserting the dead key
were updated -- a deliberate contract change, called out here because it is the one place this batch
changes a pinned expectation rather than adding to it. `laplace_diagonal` has no full-covariance
readout and correctly reports the gap; `gaussian_diagonal_exact` reports non-invariant, which is
right for what this verifier measures (the stored-parameter divergence under a global gauge, not the
gauge equivariance of its coupling energy).

## Strided held-out evaluation (`cfg.eval_stride`), branch `feat/2026-07-26-eval-stride`

`vfe3/data/datasets.py`, `vfe3/config.py`, `train_vfe3.py`, `ablation.py`, `scaling.py`,
`vfe3/viz/report.py`, `tests/test_eval_stride.py` (+30 tests), plus three test stubs widened for the
new keyword.

**Why.** With disjoint held-out windows (`stride == seq_len`, the only thing the eval path could do)
the first target of every window is predicted from one token of context, the second from two, and so
on. The reported cross-entropy is inflated by that starvation, and the inflation grows as `seq_len`
shrinks -- at `max_seq_len = 128` the starved head is a large fraction of each window, not the small
one it would be at 1024.

**What.** `eval_stride` selects the standard sliding-window evaluation for validation and test: each
window still runs on its full `seq_len` of context, but only its final `stride` targets are scored
and the overlapping prefix is masked to `-100`. Every transition is therefore counted exactly ONCE,
at `seq_len - stride` tokens of context or better. The masked prefix is context, not padding --
`train.evaluate` already runs the model on the whole window and weights it by the count of
non-ignored targets, and `targets` never touch the E-step (only the decode and CE), so masking
changes what is averaged without perturbing inference. This is the recipe in HuggingFace's
fixed-length-model perplexity documentation.

**The exactly-once contract survives.** The recorded plan was to disable `pad_final` on the strided
path, which would have dropped the tail and given up exactly-once coverage. That turned out to be
unnecessary: `pad_final` already means "cover every transition once, padding and masking as needed",
and it generalizes to overlapping windows directly. Window `i` scores transitions
`[i*stride + seq_len - stride, i*stride + seq_len)`, which tile the stream contiguously from zero;
`n` is the fewest windows whose last one reaches the final transition, and the existing tail padding
handles the overrun. At `stride == seq_len` every formula reduces exactly to the old one
(`n == ceil(transitions / seq_len)`, mask covers zero positions), so `eval_stride=None` is
byte-identical to the previous behavior. Verified exactly-once at eleven shapes including the ragged
and single-window degenerate cases, and end-to-end through a real `DataLoader`: 400 tokens give 399
scored targets at stride 8, 4, 2 and 1 alike. `stride > seq_len` still fails closed under the eval
contract (it would leave gaps no mask can recover); the train contract, which shuffles and drops its
tail, still allows it.

**One live hazard fixed in passing.** `.to(torch.long)` is a no-op for an int64 stream, so the
returned `targets` is a VIEW into the corpus tensor. Masking it in place would have silently
corrupted the stream for every later window. The masking path clones first, and a test pins that
iterating leaves the stream bit-identical.

**Wiring.** All three drivers that publish held-out numbers honor it -- `train_vfe3._select_loader`,
`ablation.get_loader`, `scaling.get_loader` -- for validation/test only; train is never strided. The
stride is in each memoizing loader's cache key, without which a sweep over `eval_stride` would be
served the first arm's loader and every arm would report identical windows. `ablation`'s
extrapolation probe uses the same stride as the cell's own metric, so the CE-vs-N curve and the
headline PPL stay one measurement at different N. `vfe3/viz/report._build_loader` is deliberately NOT
strided and now says so: it feeds diagnostic figures, not a metric, and overlapping windows would
make consecutive figure batches near-duplicates. Provenance needed no change -- `run_artifacts`
already validates `stride`/`pad_final` generically, so the eval contract lands in the run record and
a resume under a different one is correctly flagged as drift.

**Comparability, which matters more than the code.** A strided number is a DIFFERENT METRIC from a
disjoint one and is always lower. It must not be compared against the existing baselines (55.41, the
172-vs-139 pair), which were all measured disjoint. The default stays `None` precisely so the whole
recorded history remains the comparable series; set `eval_stride` when you want a properly-contexted
figure to put beside an external baseline, and say which contract produced any number you report.
Measured on a period-5 stream a small model learns: CE 0.00114 disjoint against 0.00056 at
`stride = seq_len/2`, the starved window heads and nothing else.

## B-01 re-measurement, branch `measure/2026-07-26-b01-remeasure`

`vfe3/run_artifacts.py`, `tests/test_mechanism_diagnostics_20260725.py` (+2 tests),
`docs/2026-07-26-b01-remeasurement.json`, and corrections to
`docs/2026-07-25-state-of-knowledge.md` §6 and `docs/audit-results.md`.

The B-01 fix landed in batch 1, but the measurements it invalidated had never been re-taken, so the
wrong numbers were still the published ones. The corrected probe was replayed on the two 2026-07-25
checkpoints under the production protocol -- first validation batch, eight sequences, depths
1/2/3/5/8 -- on the RTX 5090. `recompute_max_abs_err = 0.0` on both, so the probe's replica of the
kernel fusion is exact.

**K=300 corrected.** Pair (attention) share of the fused precision is **0.213**, not the published
0.298; prior share 0.787. Relative displacement is 0.297 after one step and 0.317 at depth 8, against
a published 0.227 and 0.323. The step-1 share of total displacement is therefore **94%**, not 70%,
and `cos(dir_8, dir_1)` is +0.965. The correction moves the interpretation in the direction §6 was
already arguing, only further: the belief E-step is more nearly one-shot and more prior-dominated
than the original table claimed.

**K=20 withdrawn, not corrected.** The only surviving 2026-07-25 K=20 `s_e_step=True` checkpoint
(`307.49_...`, trained 16:32) runs `e_step_update='gradient'`. The pair/prior precision split is a
decomposition of the `mm_exact` FUSION, which that route never computes, so the quantity is undefined
there rather than mismeasured. No `estep_character.json` was ever persisted in any run directory, so
the published 0.190 cannot be traced to an artifact on disk. Its displacement (0.501 at depth 1,
0.571 at depth 8) and cosine (+0.920) were recovered. Consequently the headline claim that the
attention share RISES with width is withdrawn: one endpoint is 0.213 and the other no longer exists.
Restoring the comparison needs a fresh K=20 run under `mm_exact` + `s_e_step=True`.

**A defect the re-measurement exposed.** `collect_estep_character` documented that off the
`mm_exact` route "the displacement and cosine are still reported and the shares are None". They were
not: the displacement window was populated only inside the `mm_exact` spy, so the gradient-route
checkpoint returned `None` for every field and the probe said nothing at all about it. `vfe_block`
receives the belief prior positionally and returns the converged belief on every route, so the window
is now taken from there when no belief fusion was recorded. The `mm_exact` window stays PRIMARY where
it exists, because it reads `mu_star` before the block's optional norm and is what the published mm
numbers were measured against -- verified by the K=300 figures being byte-identical before and after
the change.
