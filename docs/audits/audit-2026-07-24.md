## The Central Question: Do the Pure Paths Exist?

`CLAUDE.md` directs the audit not to litigate whether default toggles are theoretically pure, but
whether the pure paths exist. They do. `audit-implementation-engineer` enumerated 26 registry seams,
probed every registered key by construction, and confirmed that each seam's theoretically pure key is
reachable from a configuration that passes `__post_init__`.

The strongest single positive result of this audit: a configuration setting the pure key on **every one
of the 26 seams simultaneously** (`use_prior_bank=True`, `decode_mode='family'`, `gaussian_diagonal`,
`renyi` at order 1, `transport_mode='flat'`, `pos_phi='none'`, `pos_rotation='none'`, norms `none`,
`lambda_alpha_mode='constant'`, `gauge_transport='on'`, `spd_affine`, `phi_precond_mode='none'`,
`adamw`, `s_frame_mode='tied'`, `per_token`, `unroll`, `filtering`, `uniform` prior, every documented NN
exception off, `amp_dtype=None`) constructs under `-W error::UserWarning` with **zero warnings**, builds
a `K=4` model whose only parameters are `mu_embed`, `sigma_log_embed`, `phi_embed`, and
`decode_log_scale` (129 parameters, no learned readout), runs a float32 forward, and dispatches to the
closed-form kernel (`uses_kernel_route` returns True).

One seam-level exception, filed as finding H4: `lambda_h_mode` validates against a hard-coded tuple
rather than the alpha registry, so a newly registered alpha form is reachable on the belief fiber but
not on the model fiber. The three shipped keys are reachable on both.

## Adversarial Challenge (proposed critical and high only)

### Duel 1: bf16 transport degradation. Proposed CRITICAL, verdict DOWNGRADED to HIGH and rescoped.

Two independent investigators (`audit-numerical-analyst`, `audit-gauge-theorist`) converged on the
mechanism from different lenses, which is what escalated it.

Both duelists then worked from the project's own trained artifacts and reached compatible measurements
that nonetheless refuted the headline framing.

The skeptic loaded the trained phi table from a completed 15,000-step run whose phi hyperparameters
match the active config, composed it through the real `apply_positional_phi` BCH chart, and scanned the
entire vocabulary at all 128 positions (100,352 per-block vertex factors):

```
per-block composed vertex cond: median 35.3  p95 86.0  p99 123.1  p99.9 202.2  max 2575.3
frac cond > 300 : 0.0130%   (13 / 100352)
frac cond > 1000: 0.0010%   (1  / 100352)
frac cond > 3000: 0.0000%   (0  / 100352)
```

At that operating conditioning the attention error is bf16-noise-sized and rank-preserving: worst
per-row total-variation distance 7.6e-03 (comparable to `eps_bf16 = 3.9e-03`), with **zero argmax
changes across 128 rows**. The `TV = 1.000` headline reproduces only by hand-selecting the
highest-conditioned row, where 93.6% of the pairwise KLs are already clamped at `kl_max = 160`, which
makes the row insensitive to the perturbation regardless.

The defender read the time series rather than the endpoint and found `vertex_cond_max` reaching 2088 at
step 4500, 2760 at step 7500, and 4050 at step 13000 in `vfe3_runs/130.68_~phi/metrics.csv`, with a
second seed-6 run reaching 2975. These two results are consistent, not contradictory: conditioning does
reach the low thousands during training, while the overwhelming majority of blocks sit below 130.

The defender then conceded the framing against its own side: on a full 128-token sequence under the
active `causal_alibi_noself` prior with `alibi_slope=1.0` and `tau=sqrt(10)`, mean TV is 0.004 to 0.007
and p95 is 0.008 to 0.012. It also dropped one leg outright, having measured no KL non-negativity
violation (0 of 32,768 pairs, minimum raw KL exactly +0.0000).

**What survives, and why it is still high.** Both adversaries independently established the detection
gap using different evidence, and neither could refute it:

- Skeptic: `collect_phi_numerics` rebuilds the chart outside autocast
  (`vfe3/run_artifacts.py:3044-3053`; the artifact records `"runtime_dtype": "torch.float32"`).
- Defender: `_freeze_tensor` (`vfe3/model/model.py:170-176`) upcasts bf16 to fp32 on every snapshot
  tensor, and `_val_diagnostics` (`vfe3/train.py:937-967`) is `@torch.no_grad()` with no autocast
  wrapper, so the `transport.py:1766` downcast branch never fires during diagnostics.

Consequently `cocycle_residual`, `holonomy_deviation`, and `vertex_cond_*` all describe a transport the
forward never used. The run logged `cocycle_residual = 1.30e-04` at step 15000 while the in-force bf16
residual at that conditioning measures 1.5e-02 to 7.9e-01. **The project's primary monitor for its
central theoretical claim, flatness, is structurally blind under a supported and currently active
toggle, and no metric in the system moves when it degrades.** That is the basis for high rather than the
skeptic's medium: the concern is not today's blast radius but that the system cannot report when the
blast radius changes.

One qualitative consequence survives beyond precision loss: `kl_max` saturation doubles in the tail
(16 pairs versus 8 in fp64, while fp32 holds at 8), which zeroes those pairs' gradient through
`pair_mask` at `vfe3/gradients/kernels.py:559`.

**Open obligation, recorded rather than closed.** Both agents named the same gap: every archived run
used `amp_dtype=None`, so no bf16 run has ever logged `vertex_cond_max` or an in-autocast cocycle
residual. A bf16-trained phi could equilibrate at a different norm. This sub-claim is INCONCLUSIVE and
one bf16 run would settle it.

Two corrections to the investigators' framing that this report does not repeat. First, "the vertex
exponential has no float32 island" is **wrong**: it does open an autocast-disabled island and upcast
(`transport.py:1691-1700`). The actual mechanism is that its input was already rounded to bf16 at
`:1771` and its output is rounded back at `:1702`, unlike the edge exponential, which coerces both input
and output to fp32 (`:1084-1098`). Second, the bf16 factor storage is **pinned by a test**
(`tests/test_p1_compact_phi_block_transport_20260711.py:573` asserts
`factor_dtypes == [torch.bfloat16, torch.bfloat16]`), so it is a deliberate dtype-parity decision, and
`vfe3/config.py:2153-2162` already warns about this interaction on the transport modes the authors
judged dangerous.

### Duel 2: `phi_embed` zero gradient. Proposed HIGH, verdict DOWNGRADED to MEDIUM.

Two independent investigators (`audit-transformer-ml`, `audit-variational`) converged on this from
different lenses, on disjoint reach conditions.

Both duelists agreed on the mechanism and measured it. `vfe3/inference/e_step.py:1111` rebuilds
`mu = belief.mu - delta_mu`, so `belief.mu` retains gradient to the encode table while `phi` enters only
inside `delta_mu`. Straight-through severs it at `:1083-1086`; the oracle route severs it at `:1051`.
The phi substep cannot restore it, because `:1137` builds a fresh detached leaf and `:1163` calls
`torch.autograd.grad` with `create_graph` defaulted False. It reaches the optimizer as a literal zero
step: `grad_norm_phi = 0.000000e+00` and `max|phi_embed step| = 0.000e+00`, against 7.398e-04 and
1.007e-02 on the kernel route, with `grad_norm_mu = 2.643e-01` throughout. AdamW skips a `None`
gradient entirely, so not even weight decay applies.

Both also agreed the active configuration is unaffected (measured 5.508e-03 and 7.677e-03, kernel
route), and that `mass_phi > 0` restores a live path via `vfe3/model/model.py:1597` (measured 1.495e-04
and 1.786e-02). The freeze therefore requires a conjunction, not a whole config family.

**Leg A (oracle route) is dropped as a finding.** The defender found evidence against itself:
`tests/test_fullcov_alpha_roadmap_2026_06_13.py:123-145`
(`test_fullcov_unroll_oracle_grad_reaches_gauge_frame_only_when_enabled`) explicitly asserts the freeze
under `gaussian_full` with `oracle_unroll_grad=False` and nonzero gradient under True. The skeptic found
that `vfe3/inference/e_step.py:957-967` fires an unconditional route-gated runtime warning naming "the
prior tables" and the remedy `oracle_unroll_grad=True`; `phi_embed` is a prior-bank table
(`vfe3/model/prior_bank.py:481`). That leg is a known, tested, warned property with a one-toggle fix.

**Leg B (straight-through) survives.** The skeptic's counter was that the zero-warnings probe used
`pos_phi='none'`, a non-default, and that under the shipped and live `pos_phi='learned'`
(`vfe3/config.py:218`, `train_vfe3.py:212`) `vfe3/model/model.py:449-465` does warn. The defender's
rebuttal holds: that warning names `pos_phi_free` only, never `phi_embed`, and `pos_phi='none'` silences
even that while `phi_embed` stays frozen. Straight-through is also not test-pinned; the defender checked
and the two `is None` assertions at `tests/test_straight_through.py:159-160` sit inside
`test_detach_grad_matches_detach_e_step_true` (the `detach`/legacy-bool path), while
`test_straight_through_grad_flows_to_encode_tables` at `:109-118` asserts only `mu_embed` and
`sigma_log_embed` and is silent on phi.

**The decisive fact resolving their disagreement: they checked different ablation sweeps and both were
right.** The skeptic verified that `ablation.py:845-848` (the attention-entropy sweep, canonical versus
entropy-suppressed surrogate) forces `"oracle_unroll_grad": True` in all four cells, measuring surrogate
`phi_embed.grad` 5.520e-03 against canonical 5.508e-03, so the surrogate confound does not exist there.
The defender found that `ablation.py:883-903` (three `family='gaussian_full'` arms, a gauge-equivariance
study) and `ablation.py:934-935` (the `unroll` versus `straight_through` estimator sweep) carry **no such
override**, and that `ablation.py:1063` does set it. The hazard is patched per-arm from memory, not
systematically, and two live sweeps still run with the frame pinned at random init.

**Neither harness can detect it.** `ablation.py:1831` sums `parameter.numel()`, which is allocation and
not liveness, so arms report as parameter-matched while one arm's largest table never moves. At the live
`K=20`, `block_glk`, `n_heads=2`, `build_group` gives `n_gen=200`, so that table is
`50257 * 200 = 10,051,400` parameters. Both `ablation.py` and `scaling.py` call `train(...)` directly and
bypass the banner (grep for `parameter_report|dead_names|_banner` in those files returns no hits).

**Mitigation both agents confirmed**, which is why "silent" is false at the canonical entry point:
`vfe3/train.py:1983` computes `dead_names` from a real forward and backward, and `train_vfe3.py:443-445`
prints `dead under config (no grad): ...`. Measured `dead_names` on the live-shrunk config is
`['pos_phi_free', 'prior_bank.phi_embed', 'prior_bank.decode_log_scale']` for all four flipped variants,
and `train.py:677-679` logs `grad_norm_phi` as exactly 0.0.

## Surviving Punch List

### High

**H1. bf16 transport degradation is invisible to every flatness and conditioning certificate.**
`vfe3/geometry/transport.py:1766-1771` rounds the fp32 phi chart to the autocast dtype;
`:1702` stores the exponential back at bf16; `:1572` rounds the float64 inverse back to bf16, and
`_checked_group_inverse`'s three guards check only finiteness, never `U U^{-1} ≈ I`. Meanwhile
`_freeze_tensor` (`vfe3/model/model.py:170-176`) upcasts bf16 to fp32 for every diagnostic snapshot and
`_val_diagnostics` (`vfe3/train.py:937-967`) carries no autocast wrapper, so the published
`cocycle_residual` (1.30e-04 logged) cannot observe the in-force value (1.5e-02 to 7.9e-01 measured).
Measured consequence beyond precision: `kl_max` saturation doubles in the tail, zeroing those pairs'
gradient via `kernels.py:559`. Fix: compute the certificate inside the autocast context (or add a
stored-dtype `U U^{-1} ≈ I` residual assertion to `_checked_group_inverse`), and keep the vertex
exp/inverse pair at fp32 minimum, matching `_direct_link_edge_exp`.

**H2. Factored diagonal congruence has no precision island and can return negative variances that are
silently floored.** `vfe3/geometry/transport.py:2259-2265` computes the compact diagonal congruence as a
two-stage quadratic form with mixed-sign intermediates, while the full-covariance sibling at `:2082-2084`
explicitly upcasts to float64. Measured minimum off-diagonal `sigma_t` reaches -1.51e+03 at cond 3.5e3
and -1.27e+07 at cond 7.1e4; fp32 also breaks at the clamp boundary, returning -4.52 where the dense
reference gives +4.00. A negative variance is detected nowhere and is mapped to `clamp(min=eps)` = 1e-6
by `renyi_closed_form` (`families/gaussian.py:216`), inverting that key's precision weight by about six
orders of magnitude and pushing `E_ij` to `kl_max`, which then zeroes the pair gradient. Fix: evaluate in
an fp32 or fp64 island and clamp at zero with a monitored violation count. Note the manifestly
nonnegative form at `:2350-2352` is **not** available as a drop-in fallback: the verifier established it
lives in the dense `_factored_diagonal_covariance`, not the compact function.

**H3. Nothing bounds `‖phi‖` under the active M-step.** `vfe3/train.py:745-749` gates the chart
projection on `phi_mstep_max_matrix_norm is not None`, which is None; `vfe3/gauge_optim.py:71-77`
registers `"adamw"` with `requires_manifold_optimizer=False` and no group metadata, and all three phi
passes skip groups without the pullback flag; the bounding retraction (`retract_phi`, `max_norm=5.0`) is
reachable only from the E-step phi step and `e_phi_lr=0.00`. The only threshold,
`TRANSPORT_CLAMP_MAX_NORM = 20.0` (`transport.py:1257`), bounds the exponentiated operator rather than the
parameter and is set for fp32 overflow. Archived runs show `phi_matrix_norm_median` rising from 0.86 at
init to 7.0 by step 1500 and `vertex_cond_max` reaching 4050. The skeptic showed
`phi_weight_decay=0.03` does produce an empirical equilibrium (`weight_norm_phi` peaks at 1283 near step
3700 then declines to 1053), so this is an absent guard rather than observed divergence. Fix: default
`phi_mstep_max_matrix_norm` to the GL retraction's own 5.0 under `adamw`.

**H4. `lambda_h_mode` bypasses the alpha registry its mirrored sibling uses.** `vfe3/lambda_h_i.py:35`
holds a static `_LAMBDA_H_MODES` tuple and `:58-60` raises `KeyError` from it, while `config.py:1532-1533`
validates `lambda_alpha_mode` against `tuple(sorted(_ALPHAS))`. Probe: registering one new form via
`@register_alpha("state_dependent_sqrt")` is accepted by `lambda_alpha_mode` and rejected by both the
`lambda_h_mode` validator and the runtime dispatcher. This violates the mandated write-and-register
contract on one of two mirrored seams. Fix: validate and dispatch against
`tuple(sorted(vfe3.alpha_i._ALPHAS))`.

**H5. `phi_weight_decay` is silently zeroed under `pullback_group` and the persisted config still records
the configured value.** `vfe3/train.py:216-218` sets `"weight_decay": cfg.phi_weight_decay` then calls
`phi_group.update(phi_group_metadata)`, and `vfe3/gauge_optim.py:81` supplies
`{"pullback_group": True, "weight_decay": 0.0}`. Probe: `phi_embed` runs at wd 0.03 under `adamw` and 0.0

keys, tokens, and `hf_`/`sk-`/`AKIA` prefixes returned nothing.

**The no-neural-network constraint holds.** A repo-wide grep for `nn.Linear`, `nn.Conv`,

**Mathematics.** The order-4 Dynkin BCH series (`lie_ops.py:263-286`) matches the standard degree-5
expansion term for term, confirmed by measured convergence slope (order 4 gives 5.98, 5.99, so the
`-1/720`, `+1/360`, `+1/120` coefficients and bracket nestings are right). Both registered retractions
satisfy `R_x(0) = x` and `dR_x(0) = id` in both arms; `retract_spd_full` is exactly affine-invariant

quotient, and the limit is continuous to 4.3787172 against the analytic KL; verified against 4M-point
